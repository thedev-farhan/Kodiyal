Our Story

AUTHENTIC COASTAL FLAVOURS

From the coastal kitchens of Mangalore & Kerala, straight to your table in Al Nahda, Sharjah.

Kodiyal Restaurant brings you the true taste of the Konkan coast — slow-cooked fish curries, gassi, pulimunchi and sukka made with freshly roasted spices, coconut and love, exactly the way it's done back home.

From a steaming Mangalorean breakfast in the morning to spicy seafood platters and biryani by night, every dish is cooked fresh to order. Dine in with family, grab a quick bite, or order to your door.
